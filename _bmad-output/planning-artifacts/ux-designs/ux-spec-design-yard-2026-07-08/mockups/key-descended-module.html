Descended into a module

The hero of the most complex flow. Descend is not a scene swap: the canvas renders the same spec coordinates, filtered to the module's members and fitted to their bounds, with outside traffic entering through a boundary pin at the edge. The editor leash-follows to the module's YAML, and one adaptive drawer under the editor swaps its whole content by what you select. Tokens are applied exactly from DESIGN.md (post-remediation): Gateway teal, Stage violet, Store green — each held clear of the selection blue and the severity reds.
