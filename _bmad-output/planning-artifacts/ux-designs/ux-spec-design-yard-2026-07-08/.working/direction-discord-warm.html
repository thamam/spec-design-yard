Discord warm — the organized clubhouse

This direction treats the workspace as an organized clubhouse: warm dark grays separate regions by background shade rather than border lines, so every tool reads as having a home. Blurple is used confidently — the dirty dot, selection, quick-fix actions — while component-type hues stay in the same saturated Discord family so the canvas feels cohesive rather than color-coded-clinical. Chunky 8px controls, pill tabs, and pill chips keep density friendly without giving up the precision of an IDE.
