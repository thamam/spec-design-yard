Spec-Yard · design direction

Notion calm, in the dark

The workspace as an uncluttered desk at night: warm near-black surfaces, separation done by spacing rather than lines, and a single desaturated blue accent spent only where attention is earned. Type sits at a comfortable 14px with a humanist hierarchy — medium headers, regular body, muted captions — so the spec reads like a document, not a terminal. The canvas keeps Excalidraw's hand-drawn ease, restrained to sub-degree tilts and slightly irregular corners on otherwise quiet shapes.
